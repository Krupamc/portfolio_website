Hack Club

What is Hack Club?

Hack Club is a nonprofit and biggest open source community of teenagers, for teenagers. The organization is all about helping teens learn how to build and code their own projects.

Hack Club also organizes tons of free hackathons (students coding projects in social gatherings), online coding events, in-personal Hack Club school clubs, and more!
