Internship at Save Barnegat Bay:

Save Barnegat Bay (SBB) is a non-profit organization in Toms River, NJ. They are awesome, check them out! I interned over the 2026 summer, where I worked on the SBB Mesonet, the weather stations, and whatever was going around the Ecocenter! I created a mesonet weather-station monitoring and data-analysis platform. Weather observations and station status comes from Weather Underground, it stores historical records, and sends, outage, recovery, and reminder emails to different recipients. The website presents the collected data in a easy-format with dashboards, graphs, and downloadable data. The website also allows use of administrative tools for managing stations, accounts, emails, and more! The website can easily be setup for any mesonet. Try the website!
